Accessibility Bathroom Remodel in Bloomington, MN

ADA-compliant bathroom remodeling designed for safety, comfort, and independence. From walk-in showers and grab bars to barrier-free designs and aging-in-place solutions, our licensed and insured team builds bathrooms that work for everyone.

ADA Compliance & Barrier-Free Bathroom Design

An accessible bathroom is not just about meeting code requirements. It is about creating a space where everyone can move safely and comfortably, regardless of age or ability. Our Bloomington accessibility remodeling team designs bathrooms that follow ADA guidelines while maintaining the beauty and style you deserve.

ADA-compliant bathroom features include wider doorways (at least 32 inches clear), adequate turning radius for wheelchairs (60-inch diameter), properly positioned grab bars, and accessible fixtures at appropriate heights. Whether you need a full ADA-compliant bathroom or just a few targeted modifications, we tailor every project to your specific needs.

Barrier-free design removes obstacles like high tub walls, narrow doorways, and slippery surfaces. The result is a bathroom that is safer for everyone in the household, not just those with mobility challenges. Many homeowners in Bloomington choose barrier-free designs proactively as part of their aging-in-place strategy.

Walk-In Showers, Grab Bars & Non-Slip Flooring

These three features form the foundation of nearly every accessibility bathroom remodel. Our team installs each one with expert precision and attention to safety.

Walk-In Showers

Walk-in showers eliminate the need to step over a high tub wall, dramatically reducing the risk of falls. We build curbless (zero-threshold) showers with properly sloped floors that drain efficiently without a curb. Options include built-in shower benches, fold-down seats, handheld showerheads on adjustable slide bars, and anti-scald thermostatic valves for temperature control.

Grab Bars

Strategically placed grab bars provide critical support in the shower, near the toilet, and at the tub. We install grab bars into structural wall blocking, not just drywall, to ensure they can support significant weight. Modern grab bars come in a variety of finishes and styles that complement your bathroom design rather than looking institutional.

Non-Slip Flooring

Wet bathroom floors are one of the leading causes of falls at home. We install slip-resistant tile, textured porcelain, and other non-slip flooring materials that meet ADA slip-resistance standards. For shower floors, we use small-format mosaic tiles with increased grout lines for better traction underfoot.

Comfort-Height Toilets, Wider Doorways & Accessible Fixtures

Accessibility is in the details. Beyond the major structural changes, these upgrades make a significant difference in daily comfort and independence.

Comfort-Height Toilets

Standard toilets sit at about 15 inches. Comfort-height toilets sit at 17 to 19 inches, making them much easier to sit down on and stand up from. This is one of the simplest and most impactful upgrades for anyone with knee, hip, or back issues. We also install wall-mounted toilets at custom heights for wheelchair users.

Wider Doorways

Many older Bloomington homes have bathroom doorways that are 24 to 28 inches wide. ADA standards call for at least 32 inches of clear width. We widen bathroom doorways and can install pocket doors or barn doors to maximize usable space while meeting accessibility requirements.

Accessible Vanities & Fixtures

We install wall-mounted vanities with open space underneath for wheelchair access, lever-style faucet handles that are easier to grip than knobs, and touchless faucets for hands-free operation. Medicine cabinets and storage can be repositioned at accessible heights so everything you need is within comfortable reach.

Aging in Place & Why Choose Us

Aging in place means making modifications to your home so you can live safely and independently as you age. The bathroom is the most important room to address because it is where the highest percentage of at-home injuries occur. Our accessibility remodels are designed to keep you in the home you love for years to come.

Many Bloomington homeowners choose to invest in accessibility upgrades before they are urgently needed. This proactive approach allows you to make design decisions on your own timeline rather than rushing after an injury or health change.
