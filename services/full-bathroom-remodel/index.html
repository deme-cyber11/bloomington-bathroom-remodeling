Complete Bathroom Transformations in Bloomington

A full bathroom remodel is the most impactful home improvement you can make. Whether your bathroom is outdated, cramped, or simply not functioning the way you need it to, our team delivers complete transformations that elevate both form and function.

At Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling, we handle every aspect of your project — from initial design consultation through final walkthrough. We are a licensed and insured contractor with 8+ years of experience serving homeowners in Bloomington, MN and throughout Hennepin County.

Our full bathroom remodels include demolition of existing fixtures, plumbing and electrical updates, new flooring, tile installation, vanity and countertop installation, lighting, mirrors, fixtures, and all finishing touches. We manage the entire process so you have a single point of contact from start to finish.

Our Demo-to-Done Process

We have refined our remodeling process over hundreds of completed projects to ensure a smooth, stress-free experience for every homeowner.

  • Design Consultation: We start with a free in-home consultation to understand your vision, discuss layout options, and assess your existing bathroom. We bring material samples and design ideas to help you visualize the finished space.
  • Material Selection: Choose from a wide range of tile, fixtures, vanities, countertops, hardware, and lighting. We work with top suppliers to offer quality materials at competitive prices and guide you through every selection.
  • Permits & Planning: We handle all necessary permits through the City of Bloomington Building Division. Our team prepares detailed plans and ensures full compliance with local building codes.
  • Demolition & Prep: Our crew carefully removes existing fixtures, tile, flooring, and drywall. We protect your home with dust barriers and floor coverings throughout the process.
  • Construction & Installation: Plumbing rough-in, electrical updates, waterproofing, tile installation, vanity and countertop mounting, fixture installation, and all finish work — completed by our experienced in-house team.
  • Final Walkthrough: We walk through the completed bathroom with you, address any final details, and make sure everything meets your expectations before considering the project complete.

What's Included in a Full Bathroom Remodel

Every full remodel is unique, but here is what a typical project includes:

  • Complete demolition and disposal of old materials
  • Plumbing updates and fixture relocation if needed
  • Electrical work including lighting, outlets, and ventilation
  • Waterproofing and moisture barriers
  • New tile flooring and wall tile
  • Shower or tub installation with custom tile surrounds
  • Vanity, countertop, and sink installation
  • Mirror, medicine cabinet, and hardware
  • New toilet installation
  • Paint and trim work
  • Final cleanup and project walkthrough

Most full bathroom remodels in Bloomington take 2 to 4 weeks from demolition to completion. Larger or more complex projects may take 3 to 5 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before work begins so you know exactly what to expect.
