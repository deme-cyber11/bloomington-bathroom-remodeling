Vanity Upgrade in Bloomington, MN

Upgrade your bathroom vanity with custom and pre-built options tailored to your style and space. From countertops and sinks to storage and lighting, our licensed and insured team delivers beautiful vanity installations that transform your bathroom.

Bathroom Vanity Styles

Your bathroom vanity is the centerpiece of the room. It sets the tone for the entire space and needs to balance form and function. Whether you are updating a small powder room or outfitting a spacious master bath, we install every major vanity style to match your design goals.

  • Floating Vanities — Wall-mounted vanities create a clean, modern look while making the floor easier to clean. They work especially well in smaller bathrooms where visual openness matters.
  • Double Vanities — Ideal for master bathrooms and shared spaces, double vanities provide two sinks with ample counter space. Choose from side-by-side sinks or a longer continuous countertop design.
  • Single Vanities — Perfect for guest bathrooms, powder rooms, and smaller spaces. Available in a wide range of widths from 24 inches to 48 inches to fit any layout.
  • Custom Built-In Vanities — For homeowners who want a one-of-a-kind piece, we build custom vanities tailored to your exact dimensions, style, and finish preferences.

We carry both custom and pre-built vanity options at every price point, so you can get the look you want within your budget.

Countertop Materials & Sink Types

The countertop and sink you pair with your vanity make a significant impact on both aesthetics and daily functionality. Our team helps you choose the right combination for your lifestyle and budget.

Countertop Materials

  • Quartz — Engineered quartz is the most popular bathroom countertop choice. It is non-porous, stain-resistant, and available in a wide range of colors and patterns, including options that mimic natural marble.
  • Granite — A natural stone that adds luxury and durability. Each slab is unique, and when properly sealed, granite resists moisture and stands up well in bathroom environments.
  • Marble — The classic luxury choice. Marble countertops bring timeless elegance to any bathroom. We help you understand the maintenance requirements to keep marble looking its best.
  • Solid Surface — Seamless, repairable, and available in hundreds of colors. Solid surface countertops are a practical, budget-friendly option for bathroom vanities.

Sink Types

  • Undermount Sinks — Mounted beneath the countertop for a clean, seamless look that is easy to wipe down.
  • Vessel Sinks — Sit on top of the counter as a decorative focal point. Available in ceramic, glass, stone, and more.
  • Drop-In Sinks — A reliable, traditional option that fits into a cutout in the countertop with a visible rim.
  • Integrated Sinks — Built directly into the countertop material for a seamless, modern look with no joints to clean.

Storage Optimization, Mirrors & Lighting

A great vanity upgrade goes beyond the cabinet and countertop. We help you maximize storage, improve lighting, and complete the look with the right mirror.

Storage Optimization

We design vanity installations that make the most of your available space. Options include soft-close drawers, pull-out organizers, built-in electrical outlets inside drawers for hair tools, and tall linen towers that match your vanity finish. For smaller bathrooms, we use floating vanities with open shelving or recessed medicine cabinets to add storage without reducing floor space.

Mirror Selection

The right mirror ties the entire vanity area together. We install framed mirrors, frameless mirrors, LED backlit mirrors, and medicine cabinet mirrors with integrated storage. For double vanities, we help you choose between one large mirror or two individual mirrors based on your layout and design preference.

Vanity Lighting

Proper lighting eliminates shadows and makes your bathroom both functional and inviting. We install vanity sconces, above-mirror light bars, recessed lighting, and LED mirror lighting. We work with your electrician or provide referrals to ensure all lighting is properly wired and code-compliant.
