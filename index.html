Your Bathroom Should Feel Like Yours — Not Like the Previous Owner's

Most bathroom remodels in Bloomington get triggered by the same breaking point: you've tolerated the pink tile, the broken grout, the lighting that makes everyone look sick — until one day you just can't anymore.

That's not vanity. A bathroom that doesn't work — poor ventilation trapping moisture, inadequate storage creating daily chaos, a shower that leaks onto the subfloor, outdated fixtures that scream 1994 — is a real functional problem. And it compounds. Moisture behind failing tile leads to mold. A leaking shower pan leads to subfloor damage. Small problems become expensive structural problems when they're ignored long enough.

More than that: you spend real time in your bathroom every day. A space that frustrates you every morning has a quality-of-life cost that doesn't show up on a balance sheet but you feel it constantly.

What Living With a Bad Bathroom Actually Costs

The "I'll do it someday" calculation always looks wrong in hindsight:

  • Mold and moisture damage: A bathroom with poor ventilation or failing tile creates conditions for mold behind walls. Remediation once it's established runs $2,000–$8,000 — on top of the remodel cost you were going to do anyway.
  • Subfloor and structural damage: A leaking shower pan or failing caulk lets water reach the subfloor. Subfloor replacement adds $1,500–$4,000 to a remodel. Caught early, it's a simple fix during the remodel.
  • Resale value: Updated bathrooms are consistently the highest-ROI home improvement in Bloomington's market. Remodeling Magazine's Cost vs. Value report shows bathroom remodels returning 60–70% of cost at resale — and unmoved outdated bathrooms actively drag down offers.
  • The daily tax: You pay it every morning. A bathroom that works for you is worth more than most homeowners calculate when they're deciding whether to wait another year.

What Does a Bathroom Remodel Cost in Bloomington?

The honest answer depends on scope. Here are the real ranges, with what drives them:

Cosmetic Refresh

$3,000–$8,000

New fixtures, vanity, paint, lighting — no tile, no layout change. Best for bathrooms in structurally sound condition where you mainly hate the aesthetics.

Mid-Range Remodel

$8,000–$18,000

New tile throughout, new vanity, updated plumbing fixtures, improved lighting and ventilation — same layout. The most common full transformation without moving walls or plumbing.

Full Gut Remodel

$18,000–$40,000+

Layout changes, moved plumbing, custom tile, full fixture replacement, potentially expanded footprint. For bathrooms where the layout itself is the problem, or primary bathrooms being brought to a truly luxury standard.

What's NOT in these numbers: Structural surprises — mold, subfloor damage, outdated wiring. We tell you the realistic probability of finding these during the consultation so you can budget for contingency appropriately. Most mid-range remodels hit zero surprises. Older homes have higher probability.

Bloomington Homes and Climate — What Affects Your Remodel

Minnesota's freeze-thaw cycles are hard on older tile grout and shower pan membranes. Many Bloomington homes from the 1970s–80s have shower pans that are failing silently — water is reaching the subfloor with no visible sign until demo day. We check for this during every consultation and document what we find before quoting.

Older Bloomington homes frequently have cast iron drain lines, galvanized supply lines, and undersized ventilation — all of which affect what a remodel involves and what it costs. We assess all of this during the free in-home consultation and tell you what we find before anything is committed.
