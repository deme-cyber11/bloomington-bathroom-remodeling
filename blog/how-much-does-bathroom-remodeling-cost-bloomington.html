How Much Does Bathroom Remodeling Cost in Bloomington? (2026 Guide)

If you are a homeowner in Bloomington, MN considering a bathroom remodel, your first question is almost certainly about cost. Bloomington sits at the heart of the Twin Cities south metro -- home to the Mall of America and thousands of well-maintained single-family homes built between the 1960s and 1980s. That housing stock means many bathrooms are overdue for an update, and understanding what a remodel actually costs in this market is essential before you start tearing out tile. This 2026 guide breaks down real pricing tiers, the factors that drive costs up or down, and what Bloomington homeowners should know before hiring a contractor.

Average Bathroom Remodeling Costs in Bloomington (2026)

Bloomington falls within the Twin Cities metro, where remodeling costs trend higher than the national average due to elevated labor rates, seasonal demand, and the specialized work often required in older homes. Here is what you can expect to pay for each tier of bathroom remodel in 2026.

Remodel Type Cost Range Timeline
Cosmetic Refresh $5,000 – $10,000 1 – 2 weeks
Mid-Range Remodel $12,000 – $22,000 2 – 4 weeks
Full Gut Renovation $22,000 – $45,000+ 4 – 6 weeks

A cosmetic refresh keeps the existing layout intact and focuses on visible upgrades: new paint, updated light fixtures, a replacement vanity, fresh hardware, and possibly new flooring. This is the most budget-friendly option and works well when the underlying plumbing and tile are still in solid condition.

A mid-range remodel is the most popular tier among Bloomington homeowners. It typically includes new tile for the shower surround or tub area, a higher-quality vanity with stone or quartz countertop, upgraded plumbing fixtures, improved ventilation, and new flooring. Many mid-range projects also involve converting a tub to a walk-in shower, which is especially popular in homes where aging-in-place is a priority.

A full gut renovation strips the bathroom down to the studs and rebuilds everything. This is the right choice when you need to address structural issues, replace cast iron drain lines common in Bloomington's older homes, reconfigure the layout, or create a luxury spa-like retreat with heated floors, frameless glass enclosures, and custom tile work.

What Drives Bathroom Remodeling Costs in Bloomington?

Several factors unique to this market affect your final price.

Older home infrastructure. Many Bloomington homes were built in the 1960s through 1980s. These homes often have cast iron drain lines, galvanized supply pipes, and outdated electrical systems. Replacing aging plumbing or bringing wiring up to current code adds cost but is critical for a remodel that lasts.

Minnesota winters and freeze-thaw cycles. Scheduling a remodel during winter months introduces risks. Exposed plumbing lines can freeze if not properly protected during demolition, and material deliveries may face weather delays. Minnesota's freeze-thaw cycles are also notoriously hard on tile grout and caulk joints, making waterproofing quality especially important in this climate.

Material selections. Choosing porcelain tile over ceramic, quartz over laminate, or a frameless glass enclosure over a curtain rod can double or triple your materials budget. The good news is that mid-grade materials today offer excellent aesthetics and durability without the premium price tag.

Layout changes. Moving the toilet, relocating the shower, or adding a second sink means rerouting supply and drain lines. Keeping fixtures in their current positions is one of the easiest ways to control costs.

Permits and licensing. Minnesota requires contractors to hold a valid residential building contractor license for remodeling work. The City of Bloomington Building Division issues permits for plumbing, electrical, and structural modifications. Permit fees are modest but add to the total project cost. Always verify your contractor is licensed -- it protects you legally and ensures the work is inspected.

What Is Included in a Typical Bathroom Remodel?

A professional bathroom remodel in Bloomington should include the following in the quoted price:

  • Demolition and disposal of old fixtures, tile, vanity, and flooring
  • Rough plumbing adjustments or replacements as needed
  • Electrical updates including GFCI outlets, exhaust fan, and lighting
  • Waterproofing of the shower and tub area (critical in Minnesota's climate)
  • Tile installation for floors, shower walls, and accents
  • Vanity, countertop, and mirror installation
  • Fixture installation including faucets, showerhead, and toilet
  • Paint and trim to complete the finished look
  • Final cleanup and walkthrough

Be sure to ask whether the estimate covers permits, dumpster fees, and any necessary inspections. A transparent contractor includes everything in the written quote with no hidden charges.

How to Choose a Bathroom Remodeling Contractor in Bloomington

Choosing the right contractor is as important as choosing the right tile. Here is what to look for.

Verify Minnesota licensing. Ask for the contractor's Minnesota residential building contractor license number. You can verify it through the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry. Unlicensed work voids warranties and can create problems when you sell your home.

Check insurance. Your contractor should carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation. Ask for a certificate of insurance before work begins.

Read local reviews. Look for reviews from Bloomington and Twin Cities homeowners specifically. A contractor with strong local reviews understands the unique challenges of remodeling in this market, from older plumbing systems to seasonal scheduling.

Get at least three written estimates. Comparing detailed, written quotes helps you understand fair market pricing. Be cautious of any bid significantly lower than the others -- it often signals cut corners, skipped permits, or planned change orders.

Ask about warranties. A reputable contractor stands behind their work with a written warranty on both labor and materials.
