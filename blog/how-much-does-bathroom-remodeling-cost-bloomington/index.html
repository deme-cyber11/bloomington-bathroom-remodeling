How Much Does Bathroom Remodeling Cost in Bloomington, MN?

If you are planning a bathroom remodel in Bloomington, Minnesota, one of your first questions is probably about cost. Bathroom remodeling is one of the best home improvement investments you can make, but the price can vary significantly depending on the scope of work, the materials you choose, and the complexity of the project. In this guide, we break down what Bloomington homeowners can expect to pay for a bathroom remodel in 2026.

Bathroom Remodeling Cost Ranges

Bathroom remodeling costs in Bloomington generally fall into three tiers. Understanding these ranges will help you set a realistic budget before you begin your project.

Basic Bathroom Remodel: $8,000 - $12,000

A basic bathroom remodel focuses on refreshing the look without making major structural changes. This typically includes replacing the vanity, updating fixtures like faucets and showerheads, installing new flooring, applying a fresh coat of paint, and swapping out lighting. A basic remodel is ideal for homeowners who want a noticeable upgrade without a large investment. Most basic remodels can be completed in one to two weeks.

Mid-Range Bathroom Remodel: $12,000 - $18,000

A mid-range remodel goes further with higher-quality materials and more involved work. This tier often includes new tile for the shower surround or walls, a larger or upgraded vanity with stone countertops, a new bathtub or shower base, updated plumbing fixtures, improved ventilation, and better lighting. Mid-range remodels deliver a dramatic transformation and are the most popular option among Bloomington homeowners. Expect a timeline of two to three weeks for this scope of work.

Luxury Bathroom Remodel: $18,000 - $30,000+

A luxury remodel is a full gut-and-rebuild that transforms your bathroom into a high-end retreat. This can include custom tile work with intricate patterns or large-format porcelain, a freestanding soaking tub, frameless glass shower enclosures, heated flooring, custom cabinetry, dual vanities with premium countertops, smart fixtures like touchless faucets or digital shower controls, and recessed lighting with dimmer switches. Luxury remodels typically take three to five weeks and deliver a spa-like experience right in your home.

Factors That Affect Bathroom Remodeling Cost

No two bathroom remodels are exactly the same. Several factors can push your project cost higher or lower than the averages listed above.

Bathroom size is the most obvious factor. A small half-bath remodel will cost significantly less than a large master bathroom renovation. Larger bathrooms require more materials, more labor hours, and often more complex layouts.

Material choices have a major impact on your budget. Ceramic tile costs far less than natural stone or custom mosaic work. A prefabricated vanity is a fraction of the price of a custom-built unit with quartz countertops. The good news is that there are excellent options at every price point, so you can achieve a beautiful result regardless of your budget.

Plumbing changes can add to the cost quickly. If you are keeping your fixtures in their current locations, plumbing work is minimal. However, moving a toilet, relocating the shower, or adding a second sink requires rerouting supply and drain lines, which adds both labor and materials to the project.

Permits and inspections are required for certain types of work in Bloomington. Electrical changes, plumbing modifications, and structural alterations all require permits from the City of Bloomington Building Division. Permit fees are relatively modest, but they do add to your overall project cost. A reputable contractor handles permits on your behalf so you do not have to worry about compliance.

Unexpected issues like water damage, mold, or outdated wiring can arise once demolition begins. It is wise to set aside 10 to 15 percent of your budget as a contingency for surprises behind the walls.

Ways to Save Money on Your Bathroom Remodel

You do not have to sacrifice quality to stay within budget. Here are proven strategies Bloomington homeowners use to keep costs down.

Keep the existing layout. Avoiding plumbing relocations is one of the biggest ways to save. If your current layout works well, focus your budget on upgrading finishes and fixtures instead of moving them.

Choose mid-grade materials. You do not need the most expensive tile or vanity to get a beautiful result. Many mid-grade materials look nearly identical to premium options at a fraction of the cost. Porcelain tile that mimics natural stone, for example, delivers a high-end look without the high-end price tag.

Refinish instead of replace. If your bathtub is in good structural condition, refinishing it can save thousands compared to a full replacement. The same applies to tile that is in good shape but simply outdated in color.

Bundle projects. If you are remodeling more than one bathroom, doing them at the same time often results in volume discounts on materials and labor.

Why You Should Get Multiple Quotes

Before committing to a bathroom remodel, it is smart to get estimates from at least two or three contractors. Comparing quotes helps you understand the fair market rate for your project and ensures you are not overpaying. When comparing estimates, look beyond the bottom-line number. Pay attention to what is included in each quote, the quality of materials specified, the projected timeline, and whether permits and cleanup are covered.

Be cautious of quotes that are significantly lower than the others. A lowball estimate often means the contractor is cutting corners on materials, skipping permits, or planning to hit you with change orders once work begins.
