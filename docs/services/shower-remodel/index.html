Custom Shower Remodeling in Bloomington

Your shower is the most-used feature in your bathroom. If it is outdated, cramped, or showing signs of wear, a shower remodel can completely transform your daily experience. At Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling, we design and build custom showers tailored to your space, style, and budget.

We are a licensed and insured contractor with 8+ years of experience serving homeowners in Bloomington, MN and throughout Hennepin County. From simple shower refreshes to complete custom builds, our team handles every detail with precision and care.

Whether you want a spacious walk-in shower, a luxurious rainfall showerhead setup, or a sleek frameless glass enclosure, we bring your vision to life with quality materials and expert craftsmanship.

Shower Design Options

We offer a wide range of shower styles and configurations to fit any bathroom layout and design preference.

  • Walk-In Showers: Open, barrier-free designs that provide easy access and a clean, modern look. Perfect for master bathrooms and aging-in-place upgrades.
  • Frameless Glass Enclosures: Sleek, minimal glass panels that make your shower feel open and spacious. Available in clear, frosted, and rain glass options.
  • Custom Tile Showers: Choose from porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, subway, mosaic, or large-format tile to create a one-of-a-kind shower design with accent walls, niches, and benches.
  • Tub-to-Shower Conversions: Remove your old bathtub and replace it with a modern walk-in shower, gaining valuable space and a contemporary look.
  • Rainfall Showerheads: Overhead rainfall fixtures paired with handheld wands for a spa-like shower experience in your own home.
  • Multi-Head Shower Systems: Body sprays, handheld wands, and overhead fixtures combined into a fully customized shower system.

Our Shower Remodel Process

We follow a proven process that keeps your project on track and delivers results that exceed expectations.

  • Free Consultation: We visit your home to assess your current shower, discuss your goals, and take measurements. We bring material samples so you can start envisioning your new space right away.
  • Design & Material Selection: Choose your tile, glass, fixtures, hardware, and accessories. We help you coordinate colors, textures, and finishes for a cohesive design.
  • Demolition & Prep: Our crew removes your old shower, tub, or surround. We inspect the subfloor and walls for moisture damage and make any necessary repairs.
  • Plumbing & Waterproofing: We update plumbing as needed, install proper drainage, and apply a complete waterproofing system to protect your walls and subfloor.
  • Tile & Glass Installation: Expert tile installation on shower floors, walls, niches, and benches. Frameless or semi-frameless glass enclosures are custom-measured and installed for a perfect fit.
  • Fixtures & Finishing: Showerheads, handles, valves, shelving, and all hardware are installed. We complete grout, caulk, and final detail work for a polished result.

Most shower remodels are completed in 1 to 2 weeks depending on the scope of work. We provide a clear timeline before starting so you can plan accordingly.
