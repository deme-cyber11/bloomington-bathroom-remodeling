Tile Installation in Bloomington, MN

Expert bathroom tile installation for floors, walls, showers, and backsplashes. Our licensed and insured tile professionals deliver precision craftsmanship with premium materials for stunning, long-lasting results.

Bathroom Tile Types We Install

Choosing the right tile material is one of the most important decisions in any bathroom remodel. Each type offers distinct advantages in durability, aesthetics, and maintenance. Our Bloomington tile installation team works with every major tile type to bring your design vision to life.

  • Ceramic Tile — Affordable, versatile, and available in hundreds of colors and patterns. An excellent choice for bathroom floors and walls that balances beauty with budget.
  • Porcelain Tile — Denser and more water-resistant than ceramic, porcelain is ideal for shower surrounds, bathroom floors, and high-moisture areas. It resists stains and stands up to heavy use.
  • Natural Stone — Marble, travertine, slate, and granite tiles bring a timeless, luxurious look to any bathroom. Each piece is unique, adding character and value to your home.
  • Glass Mosaic — Perfect for shower niches, accent walls, and backsplashes. Glass mosaic tiles add depth, color, and a modern touch that catches light beautifully.

Not sure which tile is right for your project? We provide free in-home consultations to help you select the perfect material, color, and size for your bathroom.

Floor, Wall & Shower Tile Installation

Every surface in your bathroom has different requirements when it comes to tile selection and installation technique. Our team has 8+ years of experience handling all three with precision.

Floor Tile

Bathroom floor tile must be slip-resistant, durable, and properly waterproofed. We install floor tile over properly prepared subfloors with appropriate underlayment and waterproof membranes. Popular floor tile options include large-format porcelain, textured ceramic, and natural stone with a honed finish for added traction.

Wall Tile

Wall tile transforms the look of your bathroom and protects drywall from moisture damage. Whether you want a classic subway tile layout, a bold geometric pattern, or floor-to-ceiling stone, we handle precise cuts and clean grout lines on every installation.

Shower Tile

Shower tile installation demands expert waterproofing to prevent leaks and mold. We use industry-leading waterproofing systems like Schluter-DITRA and Kerdi membranes to ensure your shower stays leak-free for decades. From simple tub surrounds to full custom walk-in showers, our tile work is built to last.

Tile Patterns, Waterproofing & Grout Options

The pattern you choose can dramatically change the feel of your bathroom. Our tile installers are skilled in a wide range of layouts and design techniques.

Popular Tile Patterns

  • Subway / Brick Lay — A timeless classic that works in any bathroom style, from traditional to modern.
  • Herringbone — An elegant V-shaped pattern that adds visual interest and a sense of movement.
  • Stacked / Linear — Clean, modern lines for a contemporary minimalist aesthetic.
  • Basketweave — A classic pattern that adds texture and vintage charm to floors and walls.
  • Hexagon & Geometric — Trendy shapes that create eye-catching feature walls and floors.

Waterproofing

Proper waterproofing is non-negotiable in bathroom tile installation. We apply waterproof membranes behind all shower tile, around tub surrounds, and beneath floor tile in wet areas. This prevents costly water damage, mold growth, and tile failure down the road.

Grout Options

We offer sanded grout, unsanded grout, and epoxy grout depending on your tile type and joint width. Epoxy grout is stain-proof and ideal for showers, while sanded grout works well for floors with wider joints. We also offer grout color matching and sealing to keep your tile looking fresh for years.
