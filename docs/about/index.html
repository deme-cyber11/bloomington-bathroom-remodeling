About Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling

With 8+ years of experience and 500+ completed projects, we are Bloomington's trusted choice for professional bathroom remodeling. Licensed, insured, and committed to delivering exceptional results for every homeowner we serve.

Our Story

8+ Years Serving Bloomington & Hennepin County

Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling was founded with a simple mission: to provide homeowners in Bloomington, Minnesota with honest, high-quality bathroom remodeling at fair prices. What started as a small operation has grown into one of the most trusted bathroom remodeling companies in Hennepin County and the greater South Metro Twin Cities.

Over the past 8+ years, we have completed more than 500 bathroom remodeling projects across Bloomington, Minneapolis, Edina, Richfield, Eden Prairie, Burnsville, and the surrounding communities. Every project has taught us something new and strengthened our commitment to craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.

We are proud to call Bloomington home. We know the neighborhoods, we understand the housing stock, and we have built lasting relationships with local suppliers to ensure our clients get the best materials at competitive prices.

Our Approach

How We Work

1. Free Consultation

Every project begins with a free in-home consultation. We listen to your vision, assess your space, and discuss options that fit your budget and timeline.

2. Custom Design

Our team creates a detailed plan tailored to your goals, whether it is a simple refresh, a full gut-and-remodel, or an accessibility upgrade.

3. Expert Build

Our experienced crews handle every phase from demolition through final installation, keeping your project on time and your home clean.

4. Final Walkthrough

We do not consider a project complete until you are completely satisfied. A thorough walkthrough ensures every detail meets your expectations.
