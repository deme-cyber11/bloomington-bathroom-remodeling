Pricing Factors

What Affects Your Bathroom Remodel Cost?

Several factors influence the final cost of your bathroom remodel. Here are the biggest variables we see on Bloomington-area projects.

Bathroom Size & Layout

Larger bathrooms require more materials and labor. Layout changes that involve moving plumbing or electrical add to the overall cost.

Material Selection

Standard ceramic tile vs. natural stone, laminate vs. quartz countertops, builder-grade vs. designer fixtures — materials are the biggest cost driver.

Plumbing & Electrical

Relocating drains, supply lines, or electrical circuits adds complexity. Older Bloomington homes may need updated plumbing to meet current code.

Scope of Work

A cosmetic refresh (paint, fixtures, vanity) costs far less than a full gut-and-remodel that involves framing, waterproofing, and new subfloor.

Accessibility Features

Walk-in showers, grab bars, barrier-free entries, and ADA-compliant layouts are worthwhile investments that can affect overall project cost.

Permits & Inspections

Bloomington requires permits for most remodeling work. We handle all permitting through the City of Bloomington Building Division on your behalf.
