Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website bloomingtonbathroomremodeling.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site or use our services. Please read this policy carefully. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill out a contact form or request a free estimate
  • Call or text us at (952) 900-7486
  • Email us at contact@bloomingtonbathroomremodeling.com
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or blog updates

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, home address, and details about your bathroom remodeling project.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile)
  • Screen resolution and viewport size
  • Click patterns and scroll behavior

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide free estimates
  • Schedule consultations and coordinate remodeling projects
  • Communicate with you about our services, promotions, and updates
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Prevent fraudulent activity and ensure site security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Web3Forms

Our contact forms are powered by Web3Forms. When you submit a form on our Site, your information (name, email, phone number, and message) is transmitted through Web3Forms to our email inbox. Web3Forms acts as a data processor and does not store your form submissions permanently. For more information, please review the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to analyze website traffic and user behavior. GA4 collects data such as pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, device information, and general geographic location. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this data. The information collected is aggregated and anonymized. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our Site through session recordings and heatmaps. Clarity captures anonymized data about clicks, scrolls, mouse movements, and page navigation. Clarity does not collect personal information such as passwords, payment data, or keystrokes in form fields. Microsoft may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. For more information, visit the Microsoft Clarity Terms of Service.

Cookies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect analytics data. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the Site to function properly, such as remembering your preferences and enabling core features.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to collect anonymized data about how you use our Site, helping us improve our content and user experience.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how our Site performs and identify areas for improvement.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of our Site.

Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services that collect, process, or store data on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Website analytics and traffic analysis
  • Microsoft Clarity: Session recordings, heatmaps, and user behavior analysis
  • Web3Forms: Contact form submission processing
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery (Space Grotesk and DM Sans)
  • Phosphor Icons: Icon delivery via unpkg.com CDN

Each of these third-party providers maintains their own privacy policies governing the use of your data. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our Site is served over HTTPS to encrypt data in transit. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We urge you to take steps to protect your personal information, such as keeping your contact information up to date and not sharing sensitive details through unsecured channels.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us directly.
  • Data Portability: Request your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.
