How to Choose a Bathroom Remodeling Contractor in Bloomington

Choosing the right contractor is the single most important decision you will make during your bathroom remodel. A skilled, trustworthy contractor delivers a beautiful result on time and on budget. The wrong one can turn your dream bathroom into a nightmare of delays, cost overruns, and subpar work. Here is what Bloomington homeowners should look for when hiring a bathroom remodeling contractor, the questions you should ask, and the red flags that should send you looking elsewhere.

What to Look For in a Bathroom Remodeling Contractor

Proper Licensing

In Minnesota, contractors are required to hold a valid license issued by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry. This is non-negotiable. A licensed contractor has met the state's requirements for experience, education, and financial responsibility. You can verify any contractor's license status online through the state's licensing lookup tool. Never hire a contractor who cannot provide their license number or who claims they do not need one for your project.

Insurance Coverage

A reputable contractor carries both general liability insurance and workers' compensation insurance. General liability protects your property if something goes wrong during the project. Workers' compensation covers the contractor's employees if they are injured on your property. Without these coverages, you could be held financially responsible for damages or injuries. Ask to see a current certificate of insurance and verify it is active before signing any contract.

Positive Reviews and Reputation

Online reviews are one of the best tools available for evaluating a contractor. Check Google, Yelp, and the Better Business Bureau for feedback from past customers. Look for patterns in the reviews rather than focusing on any single comment. A contractor with dozens of five-star reviews and consistent praise for communication, quality, and timeliness is a strong choice. Pay attention to how the contractor responds to negative reviews as well. A professional response that acknowledges the concern and offers a resolution says a lot about how they handle problems.

A Portfolio of Completed Projects

Any experienced bathroom remodeling contractor should be able to show you examples of their work. This might be a portfolio on their website, photos on their social media pages, or before-and-after images they share during the estimate. Reviewing completed projects gives you a sense of the contractor's style, craftsmanship, and attention to detail. If a contractor cannot show you any examples of past work, that is a concern.

Warranty on Workmanship

A confident contractor stands behind their work with a written warranty. This warranty should cover defects in workmanship for a reasonable period, typically one to two years. The warranty should be clearly spelled out in your contract so there is no ambiguity about what is covered and for how long. A contractor who refuses to offer a warranty may not be confident in the quality of their own work.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

Before you sign a contract, have a detailed conversation with your contractor. Here are the key questions every Bloomington homeowner should ask.

How long have you been doing bathroom remodels? Experience matters in this trade. A contractor who has been remodeling bathrooms for several years has encountered and solved a wide range of challenges. They know what works, what does not, and how to handle surprises behind the walls.

Will you handle the permits? In Bloomington, certain types of bathroom remodeling work require permits from the City of Bloomington Building Division. A professional contractor handles the permitting process on your behalf, ensuring that all work meets local building codes and passes inspection.

Who will be working in my home? Find out whether the contractor uses their own employees or subcontractors. If they use subcontractors, ask whether those subs are also licensed and insured. You deserve to know who will be in your home every day during the project.

What is the payment schedule? A fair payment schedule ties payments to project milestones rather than requiring a large upfront deposit. A typical structure might be a small deposit to secure your spot on the schedule, a payment at the midpoint of the project, and a final payment upon completion and your satisfaction. Be wary of any contractor who demands full payment upfront.

What is your estimated timeline? Get a realistic timeline in writing. Ask what could cause delays and how the contractor handles scheduling changes. A professional contractor communicates proactively if the timeline needs to shift and explains why.

Red Flags to Avoid

Knowing what to avoid is just as important as knowing what to look for. Here are the warning signs that should make you think twice about hiring a contractor.

No written estimate or contract. If a contractor gives you a verbal quote and wants to start work without a written contract, walk away. A written contract protects both parties and should clearly outline the scope of work, materials, timeline, payment schedule, and warranty terms.

Pressure to decide immediately. A reputable contractor gives you time to review their estimate and compare it with other quotes. High-pressure tactics like claiming a price is only good today or that they have limited availability are designed to prevent you from doing your due diligence.

Cash-only payments with no receipts. Legitimate businesses accept multiple forms of payment and provide receipts for every transaction. A contractor who insists on cash and does not provide documentation may not be reporting their income, which could indicate other shortcuts in their business practices.

No physical business address or online presence. A contractor who cannot be found online, has no website, no Google Business listing, and no verifiable address is a risk. Established businesses have a footprint that you can verify.

Unwillingness to provide references. If a contractor hesitates or refuses to connect you with past customers, consider that a major red flag. Satisfied customers are happy to share their experience, and a good contractor knows that.

Choose the Right Contractor for Your Bloomington Bathroom Remodel

Taking the time to vet your contractor thoroughly will save you money, stress, and headaches down the road. A great bathroom remodel starts with a great contractor, and the right contractor is one who is licensed, insured, experienced, transparent about pricing, and backed by a strong reputation in the community.
