Bathroom Remodeling Services in Bloomington

We offer a full range of bathroom remodeling services to Bloomington homeowners. No matter the size or scope of your project, our experienced crew delivers professional results from start to finish.

  • Full Bathroom Remodel — Complete gut-and-rebuild transformations with custom layouts, premium fixtures, and modern finishes.
  • Shower Remodel — Walk-in showers, tile upgrades, glass enclosures, and custom shower systems.
  • Bathtub Replacement — Soaking tubs, freestanding tubs, and tub-to-shower conversions.
  • Tile Installation — Floor tile, wall tile, shower tile, backsplash, and custom mosaic work.
  • Vanity Upgrade — Single and double vanities, custom countertops, and modern storage solutions.
  • Accessibility Remodel — Grab bars, walk-in tubs, barrier-free showers, and ADA-compliant designs.
