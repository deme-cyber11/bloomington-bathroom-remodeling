About Savage, MN

Savage is a growing city of over 30,000 residents located in Scott County, just southwest of Burnsville along the Minnesota River. The city is known for its newer housing developments, excellent schools, and easy access to both the Twin Cities metro and the natural beauty of the Minnesota River Valley.

With neighborhoods like Southbridge Crossings, Egan Estates, and The Preserve at Savage, the city offers a mix of contemporary single-family homes, townhomes, and custom-built properties. Many of these homes feature builder-grade bathrooms that are prime candidates for personalized upgrades and modern renovations.

Whether you are upgrading a master bathroom in a newer Savage development, renovating a hall bath for a growing family, or adding accessibility features to age comfortably in place, Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling delivers the quality craftsmanship and attention to detail that Savage homeowners deserve.
