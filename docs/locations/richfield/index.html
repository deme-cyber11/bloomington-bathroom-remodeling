Bathroom Remodeling in Richfield, MN

Professional bathroom remodeling for Richfield's charming mid-century homes. We specialize in updating older bathrooms with modern designs, efficient layouts, and quality finishes that bring new life to your home. Licensed, insured, and just minutes away.

Bathroom Remodeling Specialists for Richfield Homes

Richfield is a community with deep roots and strong character. Nestled between Minneapolis and Bloomington in Hennepin County, Richfield is known for its well-kept mid-century homes, tree-lined streets, and tight-knit neighborhoods. Most homes in Richfield were built during the 1950s and 1960s housing boom, which means many bathrooms in the city are original or have only seen minor updates over the decades.

If your Richfield home still has its original pink or green tile, a dated vanity, a worn-out bathtub, or cramped shower, you are not alone. These are the exact projects we handle every day. Our team specializes in transforming outdated mid-century bathrooms into modern, functional spaces while respecting the character and structure of your Richfield home. We understand the common layouts, plumbing configurations, and construction methods used in Richfield's housing stock, which means fewer surprises and a smoother remodel for you.

Bathroom Remodeling Services Available in Richfield

We bring our full range of professional bathroom remodeling services to Richfield homeowners. Whether you need a simple refresh or a complete transformation, our experienced crew handles it all.

  • Full Bathroom Remodel — Complete updates to layout, plumbing, tile, fixtures, and finishes for a brand-new bathroom.
  • Shower Remodel — Modern walk-in showers, updated tile, glass enclosures, and new shower fixtures to replace dated stall showers.
  • Bathtub Replacement — Replace old cast-iron tubs with modern soaking tubs, or convert your tub to a spacious walk-in shower.
  • Tile Installation — Remove old tile and install modern floor tile, wall tile, and shower tile with clean, updated patterns.
  • Vanity Upgrade — Replace small, dated vanities with modern storage-rich options, new countertops, and updated sinks and faucets.
  • Accessibility Remodel — Grab bars, walk-in tubs, barrier-free showers, and comfort-height fixtures for safe aging in place.
