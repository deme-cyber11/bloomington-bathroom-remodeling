Expert Bathroom Remodeling for Minneapolis Homes

Minneapolis is a city of beautiful, character-rich homes. From turn-of-the-century Craftsman bungalows in Longfellow to stately Tudors in Linden Hills, classic Victorians in the Wedge, and updated condos in the North Loop, the housing stock in Minneapolis is as diverse as the city itself. That diversity means bathroom remodeling in Minneapolis requires a contractor who understands how to work with older plumbing systems, unique floor plans, and the specific challenges that come with renovating homes built across multiple eras.

As a Bloomington-based company serving the greater Twin Cities, we have completed hundreds of bathroom remodels in Minneapolis neighborhoods. We know the common issues Minneapolis homeowners face: outdated tile from the 1960s, small bathrooms in pre-war homes that need smarter layouts, galvanized plumbing that needs updating, and the desire to modernize without losing the character that makes Minneapolis homes special.

Bathroom Remodeling Services Available in Minneapolis

We bring our full suite of bathroom remodeling services to Minneapolis homeowners. Every project is handled by our experienced in-house crew from demolition through final walkthrough.

  • Full Bathroom Remodel — Complete transformations including layout changes, new plumbing, custom tile, and premium fixtures.
  • Shower Remodel — Walk-in showers, frameless glass enclosures, custom tile work, and rain shower systems.
  • Bathtub Replacement — Freestanding soaking tubs, alcove replacements, and tub-to-shower conversions for older Minneapolis homes.
  • Tile Installation — Subway tile, hexagonal floor tile, large-format wall tile, and custom mosaic patterns.
  • Vanity Upgrade — Modern floating vanities, double-sink setups, custom countertops, and storage solutions for compact Minneapolis bathrooms.
  • Accessibility Remodel — Barrier-free showers, grab bars, comfort-height fixtures, and ADA-compliant designs for aging in place.

Serving All Minneapolis Neighborhoods
