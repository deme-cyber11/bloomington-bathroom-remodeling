About Prior Lake, MN

Prior Lake is a picturesque city of over 27,000 residents nestled around Prior Lake and Upper Prior Lake in Scott County. Located approximately 25 miles southwest of Minneapolis, the city has become one of the most desirable communities in the South Metro Twin Cities thanks to its beautiful lakefront living, top-rated schools, and vibrant downtown district.

The housing stock in Prior Lake ranges from impressive lakefront estates and custom-built luxury homes to well-appointed newer construction in developments like The Wilds, Jeffers Point, and credit River Township. Many of these homes feature spacious master bathrooms that are ideal candidates for high-end upgrades, from spa-inspired shower systems to elegant freestanding tubs and custom tile work.

Whether you are renovating a lakefront master bath to capture the views, upgrading a guest bathroom for visitors, or creating an accessible bathroom for comfortable living at any age, Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling delivers the quality and craftsmanship that Prior Lake homeowners expect.
