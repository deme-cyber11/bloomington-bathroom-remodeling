About Apple Valley, MN

Apple Valley is a thriving city of over 55,000 residents in Dakota County, located just south of Burnsville and east of Lakeville along Cedar Avenue (Highway 77). The city is perhaps best known as the home of the Minnesota Zoo, one of the state's top family attractions, but it is the strong neighborhoods, excellent District 196 schools, and welcoming community atmosphere that truly define Apple Valley.

The city's housing stock is diverse, featuring everything from classic split-levels and ramblers built in the 1970s and 1980s to newer construction in developments near Cobblestone Lake and the Quarry area. Many of these homes have bathrooms that are ready for a modern update, whether that means replacing dated tile and fixtures, expanding a cramped layout, or creating a spa-like retreat in the master suite.

Whether you live near the zoo, in the Palomino Hills neighborhood, along Galaxie Avenue, or in one of Apple Valley's many well-established subdivisions, Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling delivers the quality workmanship, reliable timelines, and honest pricing that Apple Valley families count on.
