About Shakopee, MN

Shakopee is a rapidly growing city of over 45,000 residents located in Scott County along the Minnesota River. As the county seat of Scott County, Shakopee has a rich history dating back to the 1850s while simultaneously experiencing some of the most dynamic growth in the Twin Cities metro area. The city is home to major employers including Amazon and Shutterfly, and is well known for Valleyfair amusement park and Canterbury Park.

The housing landscape in Shakopee is diverse, ranging from charming older homes near the historic downtown to brand-new construction in developments like Southbridge, Eagle Creek, and the rapidly expanding areas along County Road 101. Many of the newer homes built during Shakopee's recent growth period feature standard builder-grade finishes that homeowners are eager to personalize and upgrade.

Whether you are upgrading a master bathroom in a new Shakopee development, renovating a family bathroom in an established neighborhood near downtown, or converting a tub to a walk-in shower for better accessibility, Bloomington Bathroom Remodeling has the skills and experience to bring your vision to life.
